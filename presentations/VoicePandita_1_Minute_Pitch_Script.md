# VoicePandita — 1-minute elevator pitch

**Target delivery: 58–60 seconds**

> In Bangladesh, a student's access to a great tutor still depends on language, bandwidth, and ability. English-first tools, unstable internet, and inaccessible formats leave too many learners behind.
>
> VoicePandita is our Bangladesh-first AI learning companion. A student can ask through voice, text, a photo, or a PDF. VoicePandita grounds the answer in curriculum context, then explains it in Bangla through text, voice, diagrams, and a prototype BdSL avatar. Offline packs and deterministic fallbacks keep learning available when connectivity or AI providers fail.
>
> We now want to move from a Top 100 hackathon prototype to validated inclusive learning infrastructure. We are seeking research partners, school pilots, incubation, and market-entry guidance. Our vision is simple: every learner deserves a tutor that speaks their language—and understands their reality. We would love to build that next stage with you.

## Slide timing

- Slide 1 — 0:00–0:17: problem and hook
- Slide 2 — 0:17–0:42: product and differentiation
- Slide 3 — 0:42–1:00: opportunity and ask

## Delivery notes

- Aim for calm confidence, not speed; pause briefly after “ability” and “reality.”
- Do not read the slide. Face the panel and use the slide only as visual reinforcement.
- If time is cut short, skip the sentence beginning “Offline packs…” and continue to the final paragraph.
- Keep claims at prototype level unless the team has verified deployment or pilot evidence to add.
